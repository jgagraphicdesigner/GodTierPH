Hold together and follow the call.

SUB LEAGUE OVERVIEW

Win the clash before hitting towers.

The Sub League group should prioritize team survival, clean wipes, and quick tower focus after the enemy team is cleared. Do not chase too deep; regroup before every new push.

Start

Confirm your party

Check the Party List before the match. Stay with your assigned group and wait for the first commander call.

Clash

Players first

Focus enemy DPS, Priests, and isolated targets. Once the enemy clash is cleared, immediately switch to tower damage.

Reset

Regroup fast

After every push, return to the assigned regroup point. Do not overextend while the next rotation call is still forming.

CALL ORDER

Simple command flow.

1

Group before moving

  • Stay with the assigned party.
  • Wait for the commander before pushing.
  • Do not enter alone.
2

Clear enemy players

  • Focus Snipers, Wizards, Priests, and Champions first.
  • Layer crowd control and AoE damage.
  • Protect Priests and backline DPS.
3

Hit tower after wipe

  • Do not split damage during a live clash.
  • Once enemies are down, everyone swaps to the tower.
  • Stop tower damage if the enemy counterpush arrives.
4

Return and repeat

  • Regroup after every tower push.
  • Rotate only when instructed.
  • Keep the team together until all towers are destroyed.

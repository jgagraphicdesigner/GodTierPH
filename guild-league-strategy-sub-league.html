SUB LEAGUE STRATEGY

The Sub League is just as important as the Main League.

We need the Sub League resources to strengthen and support our Main League parties. The goal is to control repair materials, secure MVP timing, and block enemy movement before they can support their own Main League.

Violet Mark

Mineral Respawn Area

Secure these areas when repair-material mobs respawn. Do not let the enemy collect ore freely.

Green Mark

MVP Area

Move here 1 minute before MVP respawn. Kill the MVP quickly, then return to resource control.

Orange Mark

Party Station

Hold around this area to block enemy paths toward the MVP or toward our position.

SUB LEAGUE RULES

Resource control wins rewards.

1

Never enter the enemy base

  • Do not enter the enemy base because enemies are immune to all attacks inside that area.
  • Stay around the orange-marked area.
  • Block the enemy path going to the MVP or toward our position.
2

Secure repair materials

  • When repair-material mobs respawn, immediately secure the violet-marked areas.
  • Collect repair ore from the enemy side first whenever possible.
  • Drain their ore supply and save our own ore for emergencies.
3

Move before MVP respawn

  • Move to the green-marked areas 1 minute before MVP respawn.
  • Kill the MVP as quickly as possible.
  • After the MVP is defeated, return to orange or violet-marked areas near the enemy side.
4

Priest reveal and support

  • All Priests should use Ruwach during Guild League to reveal hidden enemies.
  • Stay together in the same party for buffs, healing, and support.
  • Do not split away from your assigned group.
5

Stay in your assigned battlefield

  • If you want to participate in the Main Battlefield, join a Main League team.
  • If you are assigned to the Sub League, stay in the Sub Battlefield.
  • Follow your assigned role so both leagues can work together.
Important Reminder

Teamwork is more important than damage.

This is not just about who can deal the most damage. Do not go off alone or try to be the hero. We will not win if everyone only focuses on fighting enemies.

If we want better rewards every Guild League, follow the strategy, stay with your team, and complete your assigned role.

BUILD GUIDE

High Priest

Support build direction for sustain, buffs, Auto Heal, Resurrection, and Sanctuary coverage during enemy dives.

All BuildsGuild League Strategy

Role

  • Keep key players alive through clashes.
  • Add Auto Heal and Resurrection to your skill setup.
  • Support lane holders and rotating parties.

Build Direction

  • Prioritize survivability and support uptime.
  • Build enough VIT to survive enemy dives.
  • Keep Resurrection ready for key players who go down.

Guild Notes

  • Spam Sanctuary when Assassin Cross, Lord Knight, Paladin, or pistol Night Watch players push straight into the enemy to use their skills.
  • Stay protected behind frontline players.
  • Keep focus on party survival over damage.

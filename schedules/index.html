GUIDES

Dungeon assistance schedules.

Use this schedule to plan Time Echoes, Endless Tower, Rift Raid, and Final Mirage runs before the weekly reset.

IMPORTANT ANNOUNCEMENT

Do not let weekly attempts go to waste.

Some members are not able to use all of their dungeon attempts before the weekly reset. To help everyone clear their runs, we will organize assistance schedules for the main weekly dungeons.

Monday-Friday

Time Echoes & Endless Tower

Assistance is available from Monday to Friday, preferably around noon and in the evening.

Wednesday-Thursday & Sunday-Monday

Rift Raid

Plan Rift Raid runs during these windows so parties can finish before the weekly reset.

Friday-Sunday

Final Mirage

Final Mirage assistance will be organized from Friday to Sunday.

Late-Night Players

Message us if you are only available late.

If you are only available around midnight or late at night, please message the leadership team. We will try to organize a party with other members who are available during the same hours.

Guild Chat

Join available parties when you see them.

Please do not hesitate to ask for assistance or join any available party posted in Guild chat. Let us help each other and make sure everyone gets the most out of their weekly attempts.

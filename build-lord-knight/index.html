BUILD GUIDE

Lord Knight

Frontline build direction for holding space, peeling, and pressuring enemy DPS without overextending.

All BuildsGuild League Strategy

Role

  • Anchor the frontline during MID defense.
  • Focus enemy DPS first, especially Snipers and Wizards.
  • Hold formation during rotation calls.

Build Direction

  • Prioritize VIT and defensive gear for guild clashes.
  • Keep enough damage or pressure to threaten enemy backline movement.
  • Adjust gear based on whether you are holding TOP, MID, or BOTTOM.

Guild Notes

  • Do not play too aggressively or chase too deep.
  • Push forward only when the commander calls for Guard or a protected engage.
  • Hold the line while Team 2 rotates.

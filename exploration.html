Exploration guide.

Use this as a quick map reference for hidden rewards, travel services, quest markers, and open-world collection routes in Ragnarok: The New World.

MAP DATABASE

Clear every map with a purpose.

The map database tracks exploration chests, services, photo spots, quests, monster chests, and other markers per region.

How To Use It

Check the map before you roam.

  • Pick the map or region you are clearing.
  • Prioritize chests, Kafra unlocks, quests, and photo spots while you are already nearby.
  • Mark monster chest and mystery chest routes before farming so you do not waste movement.
  • Return to the full wiki map when you need the exact marker location.
Best Targets

Focus on permanent value first.

  • Kafra Services help with faster travel and future farming routes.
  • Exploration Chests, Guard Chests, Mystery Chests, and Monster Chests are the main reward checks.
  • Cooking Recipes, Observation Points, and Landmark Photo Spots are useful completion targets.
  • Map Quests, Exploration Quests, Monster Card Quests, and Private Chef quests are worth clearing by area.

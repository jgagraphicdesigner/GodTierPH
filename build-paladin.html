BUILD GUIDE

Paladin

Defensive frontline build direction for protecting key players, guarding pushes, and punishing enemy DPS.

All BuildsGuild League Strategy

Role

  • Protect high-priority allies during clashes.
  • Focus enemy DPS first, especially Snipers and Wizards.
  • Guard the frontline and protect key teammates during pushes.

Build Direction

  • Prioritize VIT, defensive gear, and resistance.
  • Build for staying power over chasing kills.
  • Carry enough consumables for long defensive holds.

Guild Notes

  • Do not overextend or chase kills alone.
  • Engage aggressively only when the commander calls for Guard.
  • Do not let the backline get isolated.

BUILD GUIDE

White Smith

Durable pressure and utility build direction for guild fights.

All BuildsGuild League Strategy

Role

  • Stand near the frontline and pressure enemy players.
  • Help protect ranged damage dealers when enemies dive.
  • Play around MID regroup calls.

Build Direction

  • Balance damage with enough VIT to survive clashes.
  • Use defensive gear when assigned to hold lanes.
  • Carry supplies for long fights and repeated rotations.

Guild Notes

  • Do not chase past the team formation.
  • Stay close enough to help peel for High Priest and High Wizard players.
  • Swap to safer gear when defending MID.

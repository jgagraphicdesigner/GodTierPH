BUILD GUIDE

Night Watch

AoE pressure class for clashes. Slow enemy movement, keep damage zones active, and punish stacked groups.

All BuildsGuild League Strategy

Role

  • Spam Blazing Cavern during clashes.
  • Use Concussion Grenade Lv. 3 for the Slow debuff.
  • Pressure enemy groups before they enter MID.

Build Direction

  • Prioritize damage uptime and reliable skill spam.
  • Add enough survivability to keep casting through splash damage.
  • Pistol builds should focus on Desperado + Fire Dance.

Guild Notes

  • If you have a Familiar Card, use it.
  • Stay near the clash line, not isolated behind enemy players.
  • Follow Team 2 rotation calls immediately.

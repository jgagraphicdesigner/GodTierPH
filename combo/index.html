GUILD LEAGUE TEAM COMBO

Signal. Guard. Dive. Wipe. Tower.

Use this attack combo when command calls for a protected engage. Wait for the Guard Skill, dive together, lock down priority targets, then switch to the tower after the wipe.

TEAM PLAY ATTACK COMBO

Execute only on commander signal.

Do not dive early. The combo works when the frontline, crowd control, AoE damage, burst players, and Priests move on the same timing.

1

Commander Signal

  • The Commander will signal before activating the Guard Skill.
  • Everyone must get ready and wait for the activation.
2

Guard Skill Activation

  • Once the Guard Skill is activated, Lord Knights and Paladins push forward into the enemy.
3

Jump In & Crowd Control

  • Assassin Cross: Spam AoE poison, stun, and slow.
  • Night Watch - Pistol Build: Jump in and spam AoE damage.
  • Alithea: Jump inside the enemy and use freeze and stun skills.
4

AoE Damage

  • Wizards / High Wizards: Spam Storm Gust, Lord of Vermilion, and Meteor Storm.
  • Gatling Gunslingers: Spam Blazing Cavern continuously.
5

Kill Priority Targets

  • Snipers and Champions: Focus on killing Wizards, Snipers, and Priests.
6

Priest Support

  • All Priests should spam Sanctuary.
  • Stay ready to Resurrect teammates who jump inside the enemy formation.
7

Wipe the Enemy

  • Keep the pressure until the enemy team is wiped out.
8

Destroy the Tower

  • Once the enemy team is cleared, everyone immediately focuses on the tower.

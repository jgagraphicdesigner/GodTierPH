BUILD GUIDE

High Wizard

AoE damage and crowd control build direction for MID clashes.

All BuildsGuild League Strategy

Role

  • Spam AoE skills during clashes.
  • Use Storm Gust and Lord of Vermilion for control.
  • High-DPS Wizards should also use Meteor Storm.

Build Direction

  • Prioritize cast uptime, damage, and positioning.
  • Add survivability if you are being focused early.
  • Keep enough recovery items to continue casting after regroup.

Guild Notes

  • Stay behind frontline protection.
  • Layer AoE on enemy advance paths.
  • Stop chasing when the fall-back call is made.

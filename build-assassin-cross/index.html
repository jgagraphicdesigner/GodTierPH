BUILD GUIDE

Assassin Cross

Poison pressure build for debuffs, backline disruption, and picking vulnerable enemy players during Guild League clashes.

All Builds Guild League Strategy

Role

  • Focus on Poison debuffs.
  • Keep pressure on enemy players during clashes.
  • Target vulnerable and high-priority enemies when possible.

Build Direction

  • Poison build core: Viral Spread, Poison Buster, Rolling Cutter, Venom Knife, and Enchant Deadly Poison.
  • Build more survivability with VIT.
  • Use Odin or Goibne Set when you need to stay alive longer.

Guild Notes

  • Do not overextend after applying pressure.
  • Wait for MID control before diving deep.
  • Pressure targets that are already slowed, frozen, or stunned.

Priority Loop

  • Apply poison pressure first.
  • Force defensive cooldowns or isolate a soft target.
  • Exit, regroup, then repeat with the main clash.

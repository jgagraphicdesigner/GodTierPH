BUILD GUIDE

Champion

Burst and pick build direction for removing priority targets at the right moment.

All BuildsGuild League Strategy

Role

  • Look for vulnerable high-priority targets.
  • Coordinate burst after slows, stuns, or freezes.
  • Return to formation after committing.

Build Direction

  • Balance burst damage with enough survivability to reengage.
  • Carry recovery items for repeated clash entries.
  • Use defensive swaps when holding MID.

Guild Notes

  • Do not open fights alone.
  • Follow target calls and rotation calls.
  • Save burst for targets that matter.

BUILD GUIDE

Sniper

Ranged pressure build direction for eliminating priority targets, holding safe positions, and switching to towers after clashes.

All BuildsGuild League Strategy

Role

  • Maintain ranged pressure from safe positions.
  • Focus on eliminating enemy players during clashes.
  • Prioritize high-DPS and high-damage targets first.

Build Direction

  • Prioritize consistent ranged damage.
  • Add survivability if you are being picked off early.
  • Keep enough mobility to reposition after every clash.

Guild Notes

  • Do not stand alone in side lanes.
  • Stay behind frontline players.
  • Switch targets quickly when crowd control lands.
  • Once the clash is won, immediately switch focus to the tower.

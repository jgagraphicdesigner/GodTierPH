EVENT GUIDE

Rainbow Chest plan.

Use this guide for Rainbow Chest windows, boss calls, and party movement inside Luminous Vale.

What To Do Inside

Move as a party and keep searching.

The four boss windows

  • Team up and move to Channel 1 / Line 1 before each window.
  • Keep your party on the boss so your group can secure chest ownership during the contest phase.
  • Secret-realm chests refresh around each boss area, and any player can pick them up.

The Rainbow Chest Monster

  • This is the best target in the event because the Rainbow Chest can reward a random purple card.
  • It spawns in random spots up to about 7 times a day, roughly from 10:00 AM to 11:00 PM PHT.
  • Search the full map. Do not stay in one spot waiting for it.

Recommended

Farm with the group.

  • Be in a party before entering Luminous Vale.
  • Use Peace Mode while farming, then switch to Justice or Battle only when you need to PvP.
  • Be on Channel 1 for the 10:00 AM, 1:00 PM, 4:00 PM, and 7:00 PM boss windows.
  • Spread out to search, then collapse fast once someone finds the Rainbow Chest Monster.

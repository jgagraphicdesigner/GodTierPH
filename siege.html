SIEGE WAR STRATEGY

Break defenses and hold the line.

Use these calls when the guild needs to break a stacked defense or protect our own choke points.

Attack Call

Strategy to break an enemy defense

  • Regroup first. Everyone should rebuff, eat all buff foods, and wait for the commander call.
  • Target priority is Wizards first, then Priests. Remove their AoE damage and support before pushing deeper.
  • Assassin Cross, pistol-build Night Watch, and Alithea should dive into the enemy group and cast all debuff skills.
  • Snipers must be ready to finish priority targets as soon as the enemy group is debuffed.
  • Lord Knights, Paladins, and Champions should pressure enemy Snipers and Gunners to distract their backline.
  • Wizards should continuously spam Storm Gust, Lord of Vermilion, and Meteor Storm on the enemy area.
  • High Priests should spam Sanctuary and Ruwach to reveal hidden players.
  • Machine Gun builds should keep Blazing Cavern active on the enemy stack.
Defense Call

Strategy to defend our position

  • Wizards should continuously spam Storm Gust, Lord of Vermilion, and Meteor Storm on the path area.
  • Alithea should spam Glacial Monolith and Overload to disrupt players entering the choke point.
  • Snipers should set traps along the path area and attack anyone who steps forward.
  • High Priests should spam Sanctuary and Ruwach to sustain the team and reveal hidden players.
  • Machine Gun builds should use Last Stand and hold damage pressure on the entrance path.
  • Lord Knights and Paladins should stay in front to cover the guild and block enemy movement.
  • Assassin Cross players should keep poison pressure active across the defense area.

Secret Passage Route

How to get to the Emperium

Follow these three maps when the commander calls for the secret passage route. Stay grouped, move fast, and use the portals in order.

Step 1

Enter the upper-left red portal

On Siege Map 1, use the red portal on the upper-left side. This portal leads into the secret passage.

Step 2

Go back and enter the green portal

Inside the secret passage area, turn back toward the green portal and enter it. This will teleport you into the main hall route.

Step 3

Push to the center Emperium

On Siege Map 3, move toward the middle of the room and focus the Emperium. Clear defenders only when they block the push.

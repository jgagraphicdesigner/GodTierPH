Secret Passage

Route callout

  1. The circled areas are secret passages.
  2. Move to the left-side passage to enter the palace route.
  3. Once inside, go back through the route and enter the green portal.
  4. The green portal teleports you to the main hall.
  5. From the main hall, push inside until you reach the Emperium.
